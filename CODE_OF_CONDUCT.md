# Code of conduct

Remanence is committed to a respectful, harassment-free project environment.
Be considerate, discuss ideas and evidence rather than people, and do not use
demeaning, discriminatory, threatening, or sexually explicit language or
conduct.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when conduct makes the project unsafe or unproductive. Report concerns privately
to `specs@archivetech.org`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical;
retaliation against a reporter is not tolerated.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review threads, and
other spaces where someone is representing the project.
