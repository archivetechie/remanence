# Governance

Remanence is currently maintained by the ArchiveTech Project. The maintainers
make project decisions through reviewed pull requests and record format-affecting
decisions in [docs/decision-log.md](docs/decision-log.md).

The project currently has one active maintainer. That is an acknowledged
continuity risk, not a claim of shared governance. Additional maintainers may be
added after sustained, trusted contributions; repository ownership and release
authority remain with the ArchiveTech Project until that happens.

Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Specification questions and
private conduct reports may be sent to `specs@archivetech.org`.
